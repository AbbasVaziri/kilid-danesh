# Design Read, کلیدسازی دانش

Direction recorded after the fact for the existing site. The dials describe what the site already does.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

- ENERGY 2: an emergency service still reads calm and direct. Large type and plain language carry the urgency; nothing shouts.
- RHYTHM 2: alternating photo bands, text sections and one short stats band. Section lengths vary enough to avoid a metronome.
- MOTION 1: short hover and disclosure transitions only. No entrance animation, no parallax, no looping motion. Smooth scrolling is off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

Design Read: the ink and yellow palette, Modam typeface and the key mark are the identity. Yellow is the single accent, used on the primary call action, the logo mark, one heading emphasis and the stats band. Other surfaces stay white, paper or ink so the accent keeps its weight.

One-line reasons for the major decisions:

- Colour: ink sets a workmanlike base; yellow marks the one action that matters, calling the locksmith.
- Type: Modam at heavy weights gives a Persian display voice without a second Latin typeface.
- Cards: the service, location and blog grids are deliberately uniform because each is a directory for fast scanning. The trust section breaks the pattern with a two-column split.
- Icons: Lucide glyphs are kept only where they name the thing (phone, pin, car, wrench, key, lock, siren, check). Generic trust badges were removed.
- Local areas: the repeated yellow area pills are a navigational motif, not decoration.
- Process: the four steps are a vertical sequence because they happen in order, not four equal cards.
- Photos: stock photography is labelled as symbolic in alt text and is never presented as a named team member.

No motion runs longer than a hover transition.
